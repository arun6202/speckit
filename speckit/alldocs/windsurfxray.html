Reading this. Input is the only near-measured quantity. Thinking, output, and especially the loop size are predictions — calibrate against your Windsurf dashboard, then the bands earn trust.
Credit model. Windsurf bills external models as credits = tokens × multiplier + ~20% margin, matching its published example (10M-token Sonnet 4.6 ≈ 2,700 cr) at the 250-credit pack: Opus 150 / 750 · Sonnet 90 / 450 · Haiku 30 / 150 cr per 1M in/out. Output = 5× input; thinking bills at the output rate.
Loop math. Across N turns, input re-inflates (prior output + tool results accumulate) → input grows ~quadratically; caching bills re-sent context at ~10% (if Windsurf honors API caching — uncertain). Output/thinking are per-turn × N.
Caveat. Lineup as of June 2026 — Opus ships 4.6/4.7/4.8; Sonnet 4.7/4.8 are projected (not yet released) and priced at Sonnet's tier rate; Haiku is 4.5. Credit rates are flat within a tier. Windsurf moved to daily/weekly quotas in March 2026; these rates = what overage consumes. SWE models (0 cr) excluded.
